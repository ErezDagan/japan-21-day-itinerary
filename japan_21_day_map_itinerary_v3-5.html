Japan 21-Day Itinerary — v3.5 (luggage-minimized)

Two-hub model. Only 3 full-luggage moves in 21 days (vs 8 in v3.4). Side-trips run with daypacks while luggage stays at the hub hotel.
🧳 Luggage strategy. Most Japanese hotels will store luggage for paying guests for free even when you're not actively occupying the room. This itinerary uses two hub hotels (Tokyo D1–D12 and Kyoto D13–D20) and runs 4 daypack side-trips (Kamakura/Enoshima, Nikko/Kinugawa, Karuizawa+Hokuto, Shirahama) where the main suitcases stay at the hub. Full-luggage transit happens only on D1 (arrival), D12 (Tokyo→Kyoto), and D21 (Kyoto→fly-out).
