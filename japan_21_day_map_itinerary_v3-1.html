21-day itinerary draft

Days 1-3: Enoshima / Kamakura base

Day 1
Kamakura old town: Tsurugaoka Hachimangu area, Komachi-dori, Hase/Daibutsu, sunset near Yuigahama.
Day 2
Enoshima full day: island walk, shrines, caves, sea views, seafood; keep evening flexible.
Day 3
Day trip to Mount Oyama or a slower Kamakura temple day; transfer prep for Tokyo.

Days 4-7: Tokyo base

Day 4
Tokyo orientation + Edo-Tokyo Museum/Ryogoku area if open, food evening.
Day 5
Nikko day trip: Kanmangafuchi Abyss river walk with Jizo statues; add Toshogu if desired.
Day 6
Hakone onsen day trip or overnight-style long day; prioritize ryokan/onsen time.
Day 7
Kusatsu or Minakami/Hoshi Onsen as an ambitious onsen day; alternatively keep it as a Tokyo buffer.

Days 8-11: Kyoto region base

Day 8
Arrive Kyoto; Gion, Kiyomizu-dera, Ninenzaka/Sannenzaka evening.
Day 9
Northern Kyoto: Kibune/Kifune riverside + Kurama-dera hike; add Otagi Nenbutsu-ji only if energy permits.
Day 10
Uji + Fushimi: Byodo-in, Fushimi Jikkokubune, sake district; evening Kazariya/Imamiya area if practical.
Day 11
Amanohashidate + Ine Fishing Village day trip, or swap for Arashiyama/Nijo Castle if you want lower travel load.

Days 12-14: Japanese Alps / Hokuriku arc

Day 12
Kyoto to Kanazawa; Kenrokuen / samurai district / seafood.
Day 13
Shirakawa-go day trip; optional continue/return via Kanazawa.
Day 14
Nagano Alps choice: Kamikochi for nature OR Narai-juku/Kirigamine/Hakuba Iwatake depending on transport/weather.

Days 15-17: Shikoku focus

Day 15
Travel to Matsuyama; Dogo Onsen and Iyonada/Yosan Line coastal scenery if schedules fit.
Day 16
Kochi City: Kochi Castle, Hirome Market, Chikurin-ji, Makino Botanical Garden, Godaisan Observatory.
Day 17
Shimanto / Cape Ashizuri / Kashiwajima long nature day. This is transport-heavy; consider driver/car or overnight in western Kochi.

Days 18-19: Kyushu highlights

Day 18
Fly/train to Kyushu; Takachiho Gorge + Kamishikimi Kumanoimasu Shrine if using car.
Day 19
Oita/Beppu/Yamaga/Akizuki onsen choice, or ceramics detour to Arita/Imari if that interests you more.

Days 20-21: Choose-one finale

Option A
Okinawa/Ishigaki: Kabira Bay. Best if you want beach/nature, but requires flight logistics.
Option B
Yakushima: ancient forest + Mount Miyanoura area. Amazing but weather-sensitive and deserves more than 2 days.
Option C
Less flying: Hiroshima/Okunoshima/Etajima/Yamaguchi coast, then depart from Osaka/Tokyo.
